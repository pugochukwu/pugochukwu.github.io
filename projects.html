Mastercard Cybersecurity Job Simulation

Forage · View certificate

As part of Mastercard's Cybersecurity Job Simulation, I worked as an analyst on the Security Awareness Team. I examined a phishing email and reworked it to be more convincing, then built a phishing simulation to raise awareness of the threat internally. I analyzed the results to identify which teams needed more security training and outlined the training and procedures for those teams. This gave me hands on exposure to how organizations detect, report, and reduce phishing risk at scale.

AIG's Shields Up: Cybersecurity Job Simulation

Forage · View certificate

AIG's Shields Up simulation put me on the Cyber Defense Unit, working two tasks. First, I reviewed CISA publications, researched a reported zero-day vulnerability, and drafted a clear email guiding affected teams through remediation for a staging environment. Second, I wrote a Python script to bruteforce the decryption key on a ransomware-encrypted file, an ethical alternative to paying the ransom. It sharpened both my ability to communicate a technical risk to non-technical teams and basic scripting for security problems.

Datacom: Cyber Security Operation

Forage · View certificate

In Datacom's Cyber Security Operation simulation, I worked on two tasks tied to how their team protects clients. First, I investigated a simulated cyber-attack on a mid-sized company, analyzing the attack vector, the data compromised, and the operational impact, then wrote a report summarizing the breach, the vulnerabilities exploited, the business ramifications, and recommendations to improve the client's security posture. Second, I ran a structured risk assessment of an organization's assets to identify and prioritize potential threats. It strengthened how I think through breach impact and communicate mitigation strategies clearly.
